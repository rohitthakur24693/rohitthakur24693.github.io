Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Ai_Reels application ("Ai_Reels", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by the Inside In 30 Sec Studio Team, collects, uses, protects, and handles information when interacting with authorized Google and YouTube services.

1. Identity & Application Scope

Ai_Reels is an automated educational content generation and publishing software tool designed to produce science and engineering visual explainers for short-form video platforms including YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Operator: Inside In 30 Sec Studio Team
Official Contact & Support Email: inside.in.30sec@gmail.com

2. Google User Data We Access & Collect

Ai_Reels utilizes Google OAuth 2.0 authentication to securely interact with the YouTube Data API v3 on behalf of the channel owner. Specifically, the application requests the following OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: Required solely to upload newly rendered vertical educational videos (.mp4) as YouTube Shorts and set video metadata (title, description, tags, category).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl: Required solely to set custom first-frame vertical thumbnails and manage publication timing for uploaded videos.

Data We Do NOT Collect: Ai_Reels never accesses, reads, tracks, or stores personal emails, private Google Drive files, Google Calendar entries, search history, contact lists, payment information, or viewing history. Access is strictly confined to uploading content to the authorized YouTube channels.

3. How We Use Google User Data

Any data received through Google APIs is used exclusively for the operational execution of Ai_Reels:

  • To authenticate automated publishing workflows via secure OAuth2 refresh tokens.
  • To upload scheduled educational videos to the designated YouTube channels (e.g., 30 Sec Gyaan and Inside In 30 Sec).
  • To attach localized captions, descriptions, and science/technology tags.
  • To check upload processing status directly through YouTube API responses.

4. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Limited Use Disclosure:
Ai_Reels's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In strict adherence to the Google Limited Use specifications:

  • No AI/ML Training: Google user data obtained via Google OAuth is never used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No Human Inspection: No human reads or inspects raw Google account data, except where required by law or where explicit permission is provided for targeted troubleshooting.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used or transferred for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized advertising, or behavioral profiling.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We enforce industry-standard security measures to safeguard all credentials and tokens:

  • Encrypted Secret Storage: OAuth2 client credentials and refresh tokens are stored exclusively in private, encrypted GitHub Secrets and local isolated runner environments. They are never exposed in public repositories or client-side bundles.
  • Zero External Databases: Ai_Reels does not operate public database servers or sell data to third-party brokers.
  • Retention Period: OAuth tokens are retained only as long as necessary to maintain authorized publishing capabilities. Temporary render artifacts and build files are deleted upon workflow completion.

6. Third-Party Sharing

Ai_Reels does not sell, lease, rent, trade, or transfer any Google user data to any external third party, commercial enterprise, or data aggregator.

7. User Control, Data Deletion & Revoking Access

Complete control over account permissions is retained at all times. You can immediately revoke Ai_Reels's access to your Google account by:

  1. Visiting your Google Account Permissions page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Locating Ai_Reels in the list of third-party apps with account access.
  3. Clicking Remove Access.

Upon revocation, all authentication tokens immediately become invalid on Google's servers, permanently severing all API access. You may also request deletion of any cached metadata by emailing inside.in.30sec@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical improvements or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
